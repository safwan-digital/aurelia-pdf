About PDFStow

PDFStow is a free set of 31 PDF tools — merge, split, compress, convert, edit, sign, protect and more — that runs entirely in your web browser. It is made by Safwan Digital.

There is no account to create and nothing to install. You open a tool, pick your file, and download the result.

How PDFStow works

1. You pick a tool and choose a file from your device.

2. The work happens in your browser tab, on your own device.

3. You download the finished file. Nothing is sent anywhere in between.

Privacy

Your files are never uploaded. Every tool runs 100% in your browser, so your documents never leave your device. When you close the tab, nothing remains anywhere.

So there is nothing to hide — but a web page still loads things, and you deserve the full picture:

  • Hosting: this page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub can see normal visit data (such as your IP address) when your browser loads the page. That is about the page itself — never about your files, which are not sent.
  • Code libraries: PDFStow's tools are built on open-source libraries, loaded from public code servers (unpkg, cdnjs and jsDelivr) — the same way most websites load shared code. Those servers receive a normal file request, never your documents.
  • First-use downloads: a few tools load their library only when you first open them. The first time you use OCR, your browser also downloads the English language data (about 10 MB) from jsDelivr; after that it is cached on your device.
  • No tracking: PDFStow has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising cookies. Your light/dark theme choice is stored only on your own device.
  • Check it yourself: the complete source code is public on GitHub, so anyone can verify that files are never uploaded — see the Contact section below.

Terms

  • PDFStow is free to use, for personal and business documents.
  • Only use Unlock, Protect and Redact on documents you own or have permission to change.
  • Keep your own copies of important files. Because nothing is uploaded, PDFStow cannot recover a file for you.
  • The tools are provided “as is”, without warranty. A few conversions are marked Beta on their tool pages — they are text-based, so complex layouts may come out simplified.
